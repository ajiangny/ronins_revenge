Part of the [architecture map](../ARCHITECTURE.md). Scripts live under `Assets/Scripts/<System>/`; a bare name like
`Ronin.cs` means the file in its system's folder.

| Boon pick screen + HUD slot medallions (left-edge column; hold Tab for key/name tips). Per-element art is `ElementArt[]` sized from the enum (`Portraits/Oni_Boon_Tide`, `Soul_Tide_Painted`, `Effects/Soul_Tide_N`, `Aura_Tide`, `SoulCrackle_Tide` for Water, wired by Apply Boon Art / Apply VFX), and Asagi's talk close-up is `asagiCloseup` (`Portraits/Asagi_Closeup`, Apply UI Fonts). A new boon that would bring a capstone within reach says so in the card's note, "Opens Thermal Shock" in the Duo green or Legendary orange (`Boons.Unlocks`: the capstones whose `Requires` a `RoninLoadout.Copy` with the offer taken passes and the loadout doesn't; read from the HUD's `shownLoadout`). Any offer can be turned down: X (or a click on its hint link) arms `Reject` and a second press confirms, calling the pick back with `BoonChoiceUI.Rejected` (-1), which `GameManager.TakeOrReject` logs and takes nothing for; changing the selection, a redraw or a banish disarms it. The screen is painted, not pixel art: Oni's upper body (`Portraits/Oni_Boon[_<suffix>].png`, his pixel portrait's raised-palm pose, flames in the element's colour; framed by `portraitHeight`/`portraitCorner`) holds the caught soul (`ElementArt.heldSoul`, `Portraits/Soul_<suffix>_Painted.png`, one per catcher: Akagane's copper fire and war bell, Mizore's still frost, crane and needle, Hibiki's violet storm, drum crest and tin bells) at `portraitSoulPoint`. Without the paintings it falls back to the pixel portrait and the pixel soul frames. The pixel souls (`Effects/Soul_<suffix>_0..7`, the pickups, portal icons and talk-scene fallback) are PixelLab loops drawn from these paintings, each wisp over its bell. The Spirit Bead and Sword Arts screens hold out `beadPainted`/`scrollPainted` (`Portraits/SpiritBead_Painted`, `TechniqueScroll_Painted`, GPT image) the same way, or their pixel loops. The held art floats in a dark pocket (`Pocket`, a `SoftGlow` in `PocketColour` at the swirl's heart, behind Oni) over a soft cast shadow (`ProceduralSprites.ShadowOf`: the sprite's solid pixels, read back from the GPU and placed by `textureRectOffset` since paintings are packed trimmed, blurred at a small working size and cached per sprite; laid `ShadowDrop` below it), with only a faint aura behind it, so it is the brightest thing in a dark frame (a traced outline looked sloppy on the painted edges). Behind the held soul and Oni a `SwirlUI` vortex turns in the screen's colour over a soft wash (`SoftGlow` at 0.22, so pale colours don't turn the corner white). `SwirlUI` is reusable: `SwirlUI.Create` under any RectTransform, three counter-rotating layers of `ProceduralSprites.Swirl` (a code-built log-spiral of thin, noise-bent wisps that fade in and out along their length) in unscaled time, tinted by `Colour` and faded by `Strength` | `BoonChoiceUI.cs` (built entirely in code) |

Rule: kills from `KillSource.Effect` never trigger further effects (no chain reactions). `BoonId` order must
match `BoonChoiceUI.boonIcons`.

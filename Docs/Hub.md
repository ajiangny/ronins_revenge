# Safe areas: the temple between runs

Part of the [architecture map](../ARCHITECTURE.md). Scripts live under `Assets/Scripts/<System>/`; a bare name like
`Ronin.cs` means the file in its system's folder.

Hades' House of Hades. Oni's home between runs is the abandoned temple on the hill; it is the **only** place he walks
in every direction (`Ronin.Roaming`), everywhere else he keeps to his row.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Save slots (Hades' profiles): `Save.Slots` = 3 keys `Save1..3`, the slot in use in `SaveSlot` (`Save.Use`, `Exists`, `Peek`, `Delete`). A pre-slot `"Save"` key moves into slot one once (`MoveLegacySave`) | `SaveData.cs` |
| Title START → `ShowSlots` (each slot's runs, notes, deaths, dawns; ERASE A JOURNEY → `ConfirmErase`). `ChooseSlot`: an empty slot is a new game: `ShowSchemeChoice`, then the flute intro straight into the first run (`TitleToGame`), as Hades' first run starts in Tartarus; a saved slot fades the paper off Oni standing in the temple (`TitleToSafeArea`) | `GameManager` |
| A safe area is data: `SafeArea.Config` (name, `Backdrop.Map`, `floor` and `wakeSpot` as fractions of the map, `Station[]`, each a `StationKind`, label, spot, prop art, `propAt` (where its prop stands, map px), `footprint`, `StationFx`, `onMap`, reach; the pool's `poolCentre`/`poolRadii` in map px with its cut-out `poolWater`/`poolRimFront` layers and `poolSouls` frames; `Lamp[]` flames by map px). Map pixels count from the top-left, as in the art; `Ninja/Apply Maps` copies the layout from `DefaultSafeAreas()` into the scene, since the scene's copy wins. `GameManager.safeAreas` (defaults in `DefaultSafeAreas()`, art from `Ninja/Apply Maps`) holds them; only the first is used so far. To add one, add a Config (and its map/props to `SafeAreaGrounds`/`StationArt` in `NinjaArtSetup`) and pick it in `CurrentSafeArea` | `SafeArea.cs`, `GameManager` |
| `SafeArea.Build` measures in map space (`Backdrop.GroundBounds`, worked out from the camera so it's right before `Backdrop.Start`, as after a death's scene reload; `MapToWorld`/`WorldToMap`, `MapPixel`), so props and layers match the map's pixels whatever the window crops. It lays each prop with its lowest drawn pixel (its tight mesh) on `propAt`, a contact shadow, and a world-space prompt; placeholders when art is missing (a pool disc, tinted blocks). Each frame a prop is drawn over him when his feet are above its base (walking behind it), and `Blocks` (the footprints, plus the config's `walls`: map-px rectangles for ground painted on the map, the dais's lanterns and raised edges so he takes its steps, the platform's edges so he takes its side step, the grave nook) become `Ronin.RoamBlocks`, which he slides along. The map's ghost-fires are lifted off it and drawn flickering (`spirits` feet in map px, `spiritFrames` = `Fx/Temple_GhostFire_*`), out of step with each other and over him when he stands behind one. Station effects: incense smoke (`Incense`), a pixel glint on the blades (`Glint`, `ProceduralSprites.Glint`), a breathing glow (`Glow`); lamps light the floor around them and flicker (Perlin). All code-built, so they work without art. `Near(feet)` finds the station in reach, `ShowPrompt` floats "E  LABEL" over it. Spots are where his **feet** go; `GameManager.BodyAt` converts for `PlaceAt`/`WalkTo` and the roam clamp | `SafeArea.cs` |
| The gate (`SafeArea.BuildGate`/`SwingGate`, when the config has `gateDoors`): its two leaves are cut from the map's own pixels (`Sprite.Create` on the map texture, hinged at their outer edges, under the moon's shade at `GateOrder`) over a dark passage, and swing open (narrowing and darkening, `Sfx.GateOpen`/`GateClose`) while his feet are near the Exit station or `HoldGateOpen` is set. `gateOver` (map-px rects: the beam and roof, raised far off the map's top, and the posts) are `SpriteMask`s on his order and his wisps' alone, biting once his feet pass the doorway's threshold (`Ronin.Cover`), so the map's own beam hides him as he walks out under it | `SafeArea.cs`, `Ronin.Cover` |
| The spirit pool (`SoulPool`, built by `SafeArea` when the config has a pool): light dashes drift over the water (`ProceduralSprites.Caustic`), rings spread on it (`ProceduralSprites.Ring`, one-pixel ellipses stepped through radii) and souls rise off it and off the flagstones around the temple (`poolSouls`, a soft glow without them; in front of him when they rise from nearer the viewer than his feet), and while `Koi` is on (Asagi unmet) a fish's wake crosses it: a point wandering under the water on Perlin-drifted heading, bending back from the edge, leaving a faint pale pair of rings every half second (`Swim`), the surface clipped to the water by a `SpriteMask` of `poolWater`. Oni wades when his feet are inside the water ellipse: `Ronin.Wade(depth)` masks his sprite below the waterline (ankle- to shin-deep), the near rim (`poolRimFront`) is a mask on his order alone, so the map's own rim hides his shins at the moon-shaded brightness (drawing it over him lifted it above the atmosphere's shade), a ring sits at the waterline, rings follow his steps, and stepping in or out splashes (`Sfx.PoolSplash`, `Sfx.WaterStep`, drops). `Sfx.PoolAmbience` loops while the temple is up. `Ninja/Temple/Cut Pool Layers` (`Editor/TempleLayers.cs`) cuts the water and near rim out of `Temple_Hub.png` | `SoulPool.cs`, `Ronin.Wade` |
| Entering: `EnterSafeArea(wake)` hides the HUD, clears the cover (`BlockerWall.Clear`), plays the title theme; on a wake he is placed in the pool as the dark lifts, the soul pillar flares and he climbs out to `SafeArea.PoolExit` (past the far rim), `NoteTally` shows what the run earned and `HubReturn` plays Oni's line. The meeting: on the wake after his tenth death (`AsagiDue`: `deaths >= AsagiDeaths` (10) and not `asagiMet`), `Reveal(Asagi)` puts her at the rim with her lift clip as he climbs out, and `MeetAsagi` plays `Asagi_Meet_Pool` (`Dialogue.Find`) in place of his line, then sets `asagiMet` and saves. Then `State.Hub`, `Ronin.Roaming` on, `RoamArea` = the floor | `GameManager` |
| In the hub, `UpdateSafeArea`: E / Enter / Space / gamepad A uses the station in reach, Esc opens `ShowSafeAreaPause`; a station's page sets `stationOpen` and freezes him (`OpenStation` / `CloseStation`). A station can start sealed: `Station.sealedArt` is drawn while `isSealed` (set in `BuildSafeArea`), and `SafeArea.Unseal` swaps in its `art` in place and lets its `glowColour` Glow come up. Stations: **Oaths** (the oath stone, lower left: `SEALED STONE` until `Oaths.Unlocked` (a dawn), its chains break once in `UnsealOaths` (`SaveData.oathsUnsealed`), then `ShowOaths`), **Exit** (the gate, `SetOut`: holds its doors open (`SafeArea.HoldGateOpen`), walks up to it, waits for `GateOpened`, `TravelTo` stage 1 with `inDark` removing the temple, `StartGame`), **FluteSongs** (`ShowSongs`, the Mirror of Night), **Memorial** (`ShowRecords`: runs, dawns, deaths, each boss met by kills both ways, unmet ones worn smooth; a cut-down Codex), **WeaponRack** (`ShowRack`, Hades' Infernal Arms: the katana, or its kunai skin; `SaveData.weapon` → `GameManager.CarriedBlade` → `Ronin.Skin` at `StartGame`), **Poguru** (his nest, right of the pool: `ShowPoguru` with TALK once a visit (`poguruTalked`, a `PoguruTalk` scene through `PlayTalk` with `keepHud` so the HUD stays hidden), TIPS (`ShowTips`) and REPLAY TUTORIAL ON/OFF (`SaveData.tutorialReplay`)). Poguru perches on it (`NestPoint`, `SafeArea.TopOf`) while Oni is in the temple: at once when he loads in, after his wake line when he wakes; `SetOut` sends him back into the wisp. **Asagi** (the Pond Physician, [Story/Asagi.md](../Story/Asagi.md): `Asagi.cs`, a code-built figure at Oni's pixel size on the rim stones at the pool's back left, her clips in `Config.asagiArt` (`AsagiArt`: idle, talk, lift; `Characters/Asagi/Asagi_<Clip>_<i>`, wired by `Ninja/Apply Maps`), a block of her sea-blue without them, animated on unscaled time; E plays an `AsagiTalk` conversation (`TalkToAsagi`, a `PlayTalk` scene with `keepHud`, her talk clip while it runs). `Station.hidden` (set in `ShowSafeArea` from `SaveData.asagiMet`) leaves her out of the temple and out of reach until the meeting, and the pool's koi ripples run instead (`SoulPool.Koi`); `SafeArea.Reveal` brings her in) | `GameManager` |
| Roaming movement: WASD/arrows in 2D at `RoamSpeedScale` of his speed, no attacks; clips by the nearest of eight directions (`Ronin.WalkClip`): `runLeft`/`runRight` sideways, `walkUp`/`walkDown` up and down, and `walkUpLeft`/`walkUpRight`/`walkDownLeft`/`walkDownRight` on the diagonals; a missing diagonal falls back to the run, a missing up/down to the `idle` with a step bob. Standing still while roaming he keeps facing the way he last walked (`Ronin.facing`, a `Compass` point set from each step; `StillClip`): `idleDown` (`Ronin_IdleSouth_*`), `idle` (his back) going north, and `idleLeft`/`idleRight`/`idleUpLeft`/`idleUpRight`/`idleDownLeft`/`idleDownRight` (`Ronin_Idle<West|East|NorthWest|…>_*`, PixelLab breathing idles with their stray wisps erased and feet on the matching walk's row), any missing one falling back to `idleDown`. He comes into a safe area facing south (`Roaming` turning on); in a fight his `idle` shows his back. `WalkTo` faces the way he's going the same way | `Ronin.Roam`, `Ronin.UpdateLoopClip`, `Ronin.WalkClip` |
| Art: `Background/Temple_Hub.png` (PixelLab create_image_pro 688×384 with the first temple map as style and layout reference, its original spirit pool pasted back pixel for pixel: gate top centre, pool bottom centre, a stone dais with toro lanterns on the left, a kerbed grave nook upper right, a plank platform under a broken eave lower right), `Background/Temple_PoolWater`/`Temple_PoolRimFront` (cut from it), `Props/Temple_FluteAltar`, `Temple_Rack` (64×64), `Temple_Memorial` (96×64), `Temple_PoguruNest` (48×48, an offering bowl with a red cushion), `Temple_OathStone_Sealed` and `Temple_OathStone` (64×64, the chained stone and a PixelLab state of it with the chains snapped at its foot and the vows glowing; same canvas, so it stands on the sealed one's base in both) (Pro Flash objects styled on map crops), `Fx/Temple_Soul_*` (an 8-frame upright soul with beady eyes); `Fx/Temple_GhostFire_*` (the map's own ghost-fire, cut out and animated with PixelLab v3; the painted ones are inpainted away, and the two in the pool are gone so it holds only the souls); mood `Temple_Hub` in `NinjaArtSetup.Moods`; sounds `WaterStep`, `PoolSplash`, `PoolAmbience` (`npm run sfx`) | `Ninja/Apply Maps`, `Ninja/Apply Audio` |

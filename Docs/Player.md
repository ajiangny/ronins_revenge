# Player combat: the Ronin

Part of the [architecture map](../ARCHITECTURE.md). Scripts live under `Assets/Scripts/<System>/`; a bare name like
`Ronin.cs` means the file in its system's folder.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Movement, input, screen clamp, cooldowns, lives/invulnerability | `Ronin.cs` (reads `Keyboard.current`/`Mouse.current` through `Controls`) |
| Control schemes: `Controls.Scheme` Keyboard (arrows + Space/C/X/Z, or A D + J/K/L/I, both live; slash/parry/shuriken/ultimate) or KeyboardMouse (A D, hold Left Click, Right Click, Space, E or Middle Click). `Controls.Current` is a PlayerPrefs setting (`ControlScheme`, default Keyboard), picked on a new journey's cards (`ShowSchemeChoice`), switched under SETTINGS and on the CONTROLS page (PLAY WITH), and by `Controls.Detect` in `Ronin.Update` mid-fight (a mouse button → KeyboardMouse; Z X C J K L I → Keyboard; Space never switches). Prompts read it: `Controls.Label` (HUD Tab tips, redrawn on `Controls.Changed`; Poguru's Tips page), `Controls.Glyphs` (Poguru's lesson key caps: every binding in the current scheme via `AllGlyphs`, e.g. Space or J, rebuilt on `Controls.Changed` by `Tutorial.RedrawLessonKeys` while a lesson shows; `KeyGlyph.MouseMiddle` draws the wheel, `ProceduralSprites.Mouse(button)`; `KeyGlyph.Space` the spacebar's ⎵ mark, `ProceduralSprites.Spacebar`; `KeyGlyph.Or` a plain word between caps; `CreateKeyRow(…, paper: true)` draws them in ink), `Controls.Bindings(move[, scheme])` (`GameManager.ShowControls`; `AllGlyphs` turns them into caps for `ShowSchemeChoice`) | `Controls.cs`, `Ronin`, `BoonChoiceUI`, `Tutorial`, `GameManager` |
| Kindling: every Nth swing `Ronin.Attack` calls `ThrowFireball` instead (Fireball fx frames at twice the riposte size, `KillSource.Slash`, `CutsArrows`); its hit raises `Ronin.FireballImpact` → `BoonEffects.OnFireballImpact`, a `KindlingRadius` blast. The fireball is `Projectile.Kindled`, so its kill doesn't also roll Flame Blade's blast (`BoonEffects.FlameBursts` reads `Projectile.Striking`) | `Ronin`, `BoonEffects` |
| Sword slash, reshaped by the Sword Arts | `Ronin.Attack` → `Ronin.Slash` → `Ronin.Fan` → `LaunchSlash` → `SlashWave.prefab` (a `Projectile` with a wide hitbox, `KillSource.Slash`; the plain `Projectile.prefab` stands in while `slashPrefab` is unset). With `Ronin.Skin = Blade.Kunai` the same fan launches with the kunai sprite (`kunaiSprite`, or the element's `ElementArt.kunai` from Apply Boon Art), `Sfx.KunaiThrow` and the `attack` (throw) clip: a skin only, same hitbox and `KillSource.Slash`. `Fan` runs `FanFrom` from his muzzle and, while the Shadow Clone is out (below), again from `CloneMuzzle` with `KillSource.Clone` in the clone's colours (`Shade`). | Spirit Shuriken (W / Up), 6 s cooldown, pierces a column | `Ronin` → `Projectile.Launch(..., KillSource.Shuriken)` |
| Parry (Shift / Down / Right Click), 0.25 s window; a landed parry resets its cooldown, a miss costs 2 s (`parryMissCooldown`) | `Ronin.Deflect` → `Arrow.TryDeflect()` → riposte `Projectile` (`KillSource.Deflected`), or a boss slash sent back (`KillSource.Reflected`). The riposte copies the arrow's art (`Projectile.Mimic`), wears `Projectile.ParryAura` and homes (`Projectile.Home`) on `Ronin.RiposteTarget` = `GameManager.RandomEnemy`, passing through everything but its target and blockers. With Burning Riposte it's a fireball, with Frozen Riposte a fan of ice shards (`Ronin.SpawnRiposte`, `Projectile.Animate` with `Fx.Fireball` / `Fx.IceShard` frames); their hits raise `Projectile.Impacted` → `Ronin.RiposteImpact` → `BoonEffects.OnRiposteImpact` |

| Ultimate: `Ronin.Ultimate` (append-only, kept in `SaveData.ultimate`; only `ShadowClone` so far, so the weapon rack doesn't offer it until there are more). `Projectile.Landed` (static, raised on every projectile hit) fills `UltimateFill` by 1/`ultimateHits` for each `Damage.Procs` hit on a samurai, scout, boss or Kuon; `UltimateLocked` while the tutorial runs. `StartUltimate` sets `UltimateActive`, and `UpdateUltimate` drains the bar over `ultimateDuration` only while he's fighting. Storm Shadow multiplies each notch by 1 + its fill speed (`RoninLoadout.Extra`). Shadow Clone: `UpdateShadowClone` (a darkened copy of his current frame, facing his way, no collider) shows while it runs, standing `cloneOffset` beside him at `cloneX`: on the side toward the middle of the view when it appears, crossing to his other side when that spot would leave the view (clamped like him, from `CameraShake.Center`) and gliding there at `cloneGlide`. It copies every move: the slash's fan with its Sword Arts (`Fan`), Kindling's fireball (`ThrowFireball(CloneMuzzle, true)`: no `Kindled` blast), the shuriken (`CloneShuriken`: no `ShurikenThrown`, so no shuriken boons; `Projectile.Spinning` lets it pass his cover and arrows like his) and the parry (`cloneParryUntil`; `CloneDeflect` turns plain arrows within `parryRadius` of `CloneCentre` into `KillSource.Clone` homing ripostes, never spears, seal-darts, boss slashes or arrows with an `Arrow.Parried` hook, and never counts as his landed parry). Everything it sends is `KillSource.Clone`. Its tint and glow (`CloneTint`/`CloneGlow`) lean to the Ultimate slot boon's element. HUD: a medallion at the foot of the slot column (`BoonChoiceUI.BuildUltimate`/`UpdateUltimate`, built like the slots; it is the Ultimate slot's display, `RefreshUltimate`: the Shadow Clone icon in violet, or the slot boon's icon in its element's colour with its name on the Tab tip): a `Radial360` ring fills round it as it charges and drains while it runs, the icon dims until it's ready, and when full a faint violet fire (`ProceduralSprites.Flame` tongues leaning upward off the rim, Perlin flicker, alpha capped at `UltimateFlameAlpha`) burns on its rim; its key shows on Tab ("Z  Ultimate", or E with the mouse). Hidden while Poguru's tutorial runs until its ultimate lesson (`BoonChoiceUI.UltimateShown` = `!tutorial.Active || tutorial.ShowsUltimate`, set by `GameManager.Update`); F9 fills it (`FillUltimate`) | `Ronin`, `GameUI`, `GameManager` |

All player projectiles use the `Projectile` component (slash waves, shuriken, fireballs, ice shards, ripostes) on `Projectile.prefab`; `Projectile.cs` also defines
`KillSource { Retired, Shuriken, Deflected, Effect, Reflected, Slash, Shadow, Clone }` (`Retired` was the old thrown kunai; it keeps slot 0 so saved ints keep their meaning). The Attack-slot boons (Flame/Frost/Storm Blade)
trigger on `Slash`. `Shadow` (Split Slash's forks) and `Clone` (anything the Shadow Clone sends) are lesser
blades (`Damage.IsLesser`): one point each, `Damage.Procs` is false for them and for `Effect` (no boon effects, no drum beat, no statue burst, no ultimate charge; only the Ultimate slot's boons act on `Clone`), and `Damage.Lessen` halves what it deals
to a boss or Kuon (`Boss.shadowChip`/`SpiritWolf.shadowChip` carry the half points). Damage per source is `Damage.Of` in `IHittable.cs` (slash 1, shuriken 3, deflected 2, reflected 0: a parried boss slash only hits the stance meter). How much of a boss's stance meter a hit fills is `Damage.Stance` beside it (slash 0.005, lesser blades half, shuriken 0.1, deflected 0.02, reflected 0.15, effects 0), or `Damage.SetPieceStance` (0.35) for a parried set piece: `Arrow.MakeReflectable(target, setPiece: true)` (Utsusemi's hour cleave, Kagetsune's bell and coffin, Shirogane's Fang) → `Ronin.Deflect` copies it to `Projectile.SetPiece`, which `Boss.TakeHit` reads through `Projectile.Striking`; `Ronin.StanceBoost` adds a quarter per upgrade held for that attack.
Anything the player can damage implements `IHittable.TakeHit(int, KillSource)`: `Samurai`, `Scout`, `Boss`.

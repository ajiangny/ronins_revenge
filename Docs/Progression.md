# Across runs: save, dialogue, Flute Songs and Oaths

Part of the [architecture map](../ARCHITECTURE.md). Scripts live under `Assets/Scripts/<System>/`; a bare name like
`Ronin.cs` means the file in its system's folder.

Hades' loop: what happened in earlier runs changes what is said and how strong Oni starts. Research and numbers in
[Story/DIALOGUE_RESEARCH.md](../Story/DIALOGUE_RESEARCH.md) and [ECONOMY.md](ECONOMY.md).

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| The save: runs started and cleared, deaths per boss (`deathsTo`) and wins (`winsVs`) by `BossKind`, how the last run ended (`lastRunCleared`, `lastKiller`, `lastKillerSecondPhase`: the killing boss was in its second phase, from `Boss.SecondPhase`; `lastStage`, the stage he was on), the bosses freed (`freed` by `BossKind`: `Save.BossFelled` sets it when `Dialogue.Freeing` holds, her `Dialogue.FreeingLine` heard in an earlier run), `hubPending`, conversations heard (`seen`/`seenRun`, the run each was last heard in), the repeatables' `shuffleBag`, `embers` (the Spirit Notes, named for their old Soul Embers so saves keep them), song `ranks` and `songsOff`, `relics` and `relicsSpent` (by `Relic`: Warlord's Seals and Kite Silk held and spent), and Poguru's tutorial flags `tutorialSettled`/`tutorialReplay`. One JSON string per save slot in `PlayerPrefs` (`"Save1"`..`"Save3"`, see Safe areas), cached in `Save.Data` (reset on entering Play mode, which has no domain reload) | `SaveData.cs` (`Save.BeginRun` in `StartGame`, `Save.EndRun(cleared, killer)` in `EndGame`, `Save.BossFelled` in `BossFell`, `Save.Write` also in `Reload`) |
| Reactive dialogue: a `Conversation` (id, speaker, `DialogueEvent`, `Tier` Priority / Story / Reactive / Repeatable, `Req` requirements, `Cooldown` in runs, and either `Lines` or `BuiltIn`, the boss's own `Line()` and clip). `Dialogue.Pick` filters by `Blocked` (heard, cooling down, a `Req` failing), takes the best tier with anything left, the first Priority/Story/Reactive one written (Priority, the major events, plays once like Story and outranks it) or a Repeatable from the bag, and marks it heard. No dice in eligibility, so `Explain` is repeatable. Requirements are helpers (`Seen`, `NotSeen`, `LastKiller`, `LastKillerSecondPhase`, `Deaths`, `DeathsTo`, `WinsVs`, `OfElement`, `Holds`, `HoldsDuo`, `Felled` (the boss in `DialogueContext.Boss`), `Met`, `DiedTo` / `DeathsBy` / `Streak` (the run's last blow, below), `DiedToAttack(ids…)` / `DeathsByAttack(id, n)` (the boss attack that struck it), `OffersBoon`, `NextBoss`, `Most` / `Rival` (another soul's element leads), `LastRunElement`, `AtStage(stage, round)` (`DialogueContext.Stage`/`Round`, from 1), `HeatOn`, the boss arcs' `DiedOnStage`, `GotPast`, `OniAhead` / `OniBehind`, `Freed` / `NotFreed` / `AnyFreed` / `AllFreed`, `FreesNow` and `Untouched` (`DialogueContext.Untouched`, from `GameManager.bossHits`; [Story/BOSS_ARCS.md](../Story/BOSS_ARCS.md)), and the firsts below: `Got`, `NotGot`, `GotWithin(key, runs)`, `JustGot`); `CanPick` checks without marking anything heard; `Pick` can be limited to a tier range so one event's Story lines can be tried before another's; `Fill` writes `{Deaths}` in words. Poguru's events: `Tutorial` (never picked: `Dialogue.Find(id, mark)` plays them by id and marks them heard), `PoguruRound`, `PoguruSoul`, `PoguruBoss`, `PoguruWake` (his roasts, `Dialogue.Roast`, then the gentler wake lines), `PoguruTalk`, `PoguruStage` (a new stage's first rounds, tried before `PoguruRound`; none written now), `PoguruFirst` (firsts, below), `PoguruScout` (a kite scout appears), `PoguruLowLife` (down to the last heart, once a run, outside boss fights), `PoguruBossAhead` (the boss's path is offered; `NextBoss` is the stage's boss); `GameManager.PoguruBark` plays every line, Oni's over his own head. The souls' boon screens (`GameManager.OfferBoons` → `SoulTalk`, in Story/Souls.md's order): `Legendary`, `Duo` (speaker `"Duo"`), `BossFelled`, `SoulPickup` Story, `BossAhead`, `SoulJealous`, then `SoulPickup`'s reactions and greetings; each talk opens on the soul's sting (`StingFor`, `Sfx.SoulSting*`). `SoulHub`: a soul's line in the temple (`HubReturn`) when Oni has none. The run's last blow: `Ronin.TakeHit(contact, DeathCause)` → `Ronin.LastHitCause` (arrows carry `Arrow.Cause`: Arrow, Spear via `MakeUnbreakable(Sprite)`, GhostArrow for Yurei; dives pass Diver; the army landing is Invasion; any hit in a boss fight is Boss) → `Save.EndRun` (`lastDeathCause`, `deathsByCause`, `deathStreak`, `lastRunElement`). Which boss attack it was: `Boss.Attack`, an id like `Utsusemi_HyperBeam`, set by `Boss.Attacking(id, routine)` for each step of a move and anything it yields on (put back after each step, so Shirogane's loop and Kuon's `Hunt` never swap names; `Launch` keeps the tag, a plain `StartCoroutine` doesn't after its first step). Each boss runs its moves through `As(move, …)`, so the default id is `<Boss>_<Move>` (`Benibana_Iaido`, `Shirogane_SpiritLances`, `Kagetsune_Toll`…); `Boss.Shoot` stamps it on the shot (`Arrow.Attack`) and `Boss.Strike(contact, attack)` / `HitIfCrossed(…, attack)` on direct blows, where an explicit id names a part of a move: `Utsusemi_HyperBeam` (only the sweep a rock shelters him from), `Utsusemi_Overload` (its wild lasers), `Utsusemi_Rockfall`, `Utsusemi_SwordLaser` (the Heart Lantern's and Midnight's beams, `BeamHit`'s default), `Utsusemi_SwordThrow`, `Utsusemi_ThirteenthHour`, `Utsusemi_PendulumKick`, `Shirogane_KuonMaw` (every heavy `MawBeam`: Spirit Maw, Eclipse Fang, Frozen Howl), `Kagetsune_SpiritHyperBeam`, `Kagetsune_Levy` (his levy reaching the row). A summoned soul's blows carry its own boss's name. `Ronin.TakeHit(contact, cause, attack)` → `Ronin.LastHitAttack` → `Save.EndRun(…, attack)` → `lastDeathAttack` (empty for the army, a clear, or a blow no move claimed) and `deathAttacks`/`deathAttackCounts` (parallel lists, `SaveData.DeathsToAttack`); older saves load with them empty | `Dialogue.cs` (`Dialogue.All` holds every conversation; keep it in step with `Story/`) |
| Where they play: `BeginBoss` picks `BossIntro`, `PlayPhaseCard` picks `BossPhase` (with `lives` and `untouched`; a lone boss line, built-in or written, is spoken by the card itself, and only an exchange with Oni plays as a scene), `BossFell` picks `BossDefeat` (none written plays the boss's own line, `Unwritten`), and `OfferBoons` picks the soul's `SoulPickup` (`SoulOf(element)`) on that element's first screen of the run (`metThisRun`) and shows the cards once its scene ends (`GreetThenChoose` → `ShowBoons`). All four play through `PlayScene` (below). `OnRoninHit` picks `KilledOni` from the killing boss (a subtitle; its line is also the end screen text); `EnterSafeArea` runs `HubReturn` when `hubPending`, as he climbs out of the temple's pool (a plain subtitle; `Say` still frames his close-up beside his words if one plays on the title page). `Boss.Say(id)` → `OnBossSaid` plays a `DialogueEvent.Echo` line mid-fight by id (Kagetsune's summons by arc), voiced and subtitled. `PlayConversation` → `Say` plays lines as subtitles, voiced if `VoiceLibrary` has `Conversation.VoiceId(i)`, and marks them full lines so barks wait | `GameManager` |
| Firsts: everything had or met for the first time, so the cast can bring it up. `SaveData.firsts` / `firstsRun` (parallel lists, like `seen`) hold `Firsts` keys (`Item(PathReward)`, `Relic(Relic)`, `Enemy(SamuraiKind)`, `Boss`, `Soul(Element)`, `Boon(BoonId)`, and `Divers`, `Scout`, `Asagi`, `Legendary`, `Duo`, `SongBought`, `OathsUnsealed`, `OathSworn`, `KunaiSkin`) and the run each came in; `Save.First(key)` records one and is true only the first time. `Save.Backfill` (in `Parse`) records at run 0 what an older save already shows (bosses fought, souls' meetings heard, `asagiMet`, relics, songs, oaths, the kunai, the old stage lines heard), so `GotWithin`, which needs a run above 0, never treats it as news. Recorded at: `GrantReward` (the soul's element; Spring and Notes), `OfferBead` / `OfferThread` / `OfferArts`, `GainRelic`, the scout's spawn, round start (`NewEnemies`: each `SamuraiKind` in the army), `SamuraiArmy.DiveStarted`, the boss intro, `TakeBoon` (the boon, and any Duo or Legendary), `MeetAsagi`, and in the temple the song bought, the oath stone unsealed, an oath sworn and the kunai picked. Nothing is recorded in the tutorial. Poguru's word on the news is `DialogueEvent.PoguruFirst` (Priority lines on `JustGot`, `DialogueContext.Just`; no repeatable, so nothing new means silence): `FirstBark(key)` pops him out in the field (a first he has a word for but can't say yet, because he's out or a line is playing, stays unrecorded until it comes up again; `NewEnemies` announces one new kind a round and leaves the rest for later armies), `FirstThen(key, show)` plays it as a talk over the frozen field before the bead, thread or Sword Arts screen opens. At his nest his `News` talks (Priority `PoguruTalk`, `GotWithin`) come before everything else he says: the temple's unlocks for one run, Asagi for two, items, relics, Legendaries and Duos for three. The souls and bosses answer firsts with `Got` | `SaveData.cs`, `Dialogue.cs`, `GameManager.Talk.cs` |
| Talk scenes (Hades-style): `PlayScene(conversation)` is `PlayTalk(conversation, default)`; `PlayTalk(said, TalkOptions { keepHud, dim, poguruRight, onLine })` builds the lines (`LinesOf`), and with `keepHud` leaves the HUD exactly as it was (the tutorial's pointing, talks over the boon screen, the temple), `dim` lightens the field, `poguruRight` stands Poguru on the right and Oni on the left, and `onLine(i)` runs as each line comes up (`DialogueScene.Line.shown`). `DialogueScene.Overlay` sits over the plate and figures for what a talk lifts out of the dark, and `Advance()` steps a line for test harnesses. It waits out a hit-stop, sets `State.Dialogue` and `timeScale` 0, hides the HUD and boss bar (`GameUI.HideForScene`), and turns the conversation into `DialogueScene.Line`s: a `BuiltIn` boss line first in its own clip (`BossVoice.Take`), then the written lines with their `VoiceLibrary` clips (a built-in line counts as line 0 of `VoiceId`). `SpeakerFor(name)` stands each speaker: Oni at the right (`oniCloseup`), the boss at the left with its `VoiceFor` close-up (its phase close-up and epithet once `SecondPhase`, drawn at `SceneArtScale` of the card size), a soul at the left with its painting (`akaganeCloseup`…, framed by its eye point) or its soul frames. `DialogueScene.Play` dims the field, lights the speaker and drops the listener back, masks each half for the soul-glow and each painting in its own `Frame` mask (feathered where a painting's canvas edge would show, cut edges pushed off screen, a painting shorter than the screen drawn at screen height so it covers top to bottom, except the souls (`mayStandShort`), drawn at `SoulSpeaker`'s `scale` so their faces match Oni's with their tops feathered away), types each line under an ink name tab on the speaker's side, and advances on Space / Enter / click / South after a 0.3 s guard | `DialogueScene.cs`, `GameManager` |
| Voices for written lines: `VoiceLibrary` (every clip in `Audio/Voice` by file name), built by `Ninja/Apply Voice Lines`, which also logs the lines still unvoiced. Oni's lines (hill, answers, soul meetings, `Oni_NotYet`) play through `GameManager.oniVoice`, a filter-free source beside `BossVoice`, the souls' through `soulVoice` (`CreateSoulVoice`: a light chorus and short echo, their shared coffin sound) and Poguru's through `poguruVoice` (`CreatePoguruVoice`, a light quick chorus); `VoiceOf(speaker)` picks the source per line for `DialogueScene.Line.source` and `Say`. An unvoiced intro line stays on the card long enough to read | `VoiceLibrary.cs`, `Editor/VoiceSetup.cs`, `GameUI.PlayBossIntro` |
| The souls behind the boons (Akagane, Mizore, Hibiki, Asagi): their `SoulPickup`, Duo, Legendary, jealousy, boss and temple conversations are in `Dialogue.All`. Asagi (`Element.Tide`, screen name Water) is the koi of the temple pond and joins late: `SaveData.asagiMet` is set by her meeting scene (`Asagi_Meet_Pool`, `DialogueEvent.AsagiTalk`) on the wake after the tenth death, and until then `GameManager.AvailableElements` leaves Water out of the soul and path rolls (the first soul after the meeting is always hers, `SaveData.asagiSoulOwed`); her station at the pool's rim plays the `Asagi_Pond_*` lines | [Story/Souls.md](../Story/Souls.md), [Story/Asagi.md](../Story/Asagi.md) |
| Spirit Notes (`Upgrades.PerSamurai`, `PerScout`, `ForBoss`) go into the save as they're earned (`GameManager.Earn`, `Upgrades.AddNotes`); the end screen tallies `notesThisRun` | `GameManager` |
| Flute Songs: `UpgradeId` (append-only: saves index ranks by it; `Song.Retired` hides one and `RefundRetired` returns its notes, as for Soul Pull), `Song` (name, max rank, base cost, `Effect` text, `Needs`: the Warlord's Seals or Kite Silk a rank also asks for), `Price` (base × rank + ⌊10 × 1.1^ranks bought⌋), `Buy` (`CanBuy` checks `HasRelicsFor`), `Spent`/`RefundAll` (relics come back from `relicsSpent`), `Toggle` (maxed songs only), `Rank` (0 while off). Applied once a run, on top of the scene values: `Ronin.LearnSongs` (parry window, speed, swing, shuriken, missed parry, invulnerability, Cutting Wind's `slashSpeed`, Wide Guard's `parryRadius`, which `SamuraiArmy` also adds to its dive `parryReach`), and in `GameManager` Iron Will's life (`StartGame`), Fate's Thread (`redrawsLeft`, `BoonChoiceUI.OfferRedraw`, R) and Banishing Bell (`banishesLeft`, `OfferBanish`, B; the run's `banished` set is skipped by `Boons.Draw`), Third Path (`RollPortals`), Pond's Blessing (`BeadThenNextStage`), Ancestral Lore (`GrantReward`) and Second Dawn (`OnRoninHit`, `dawnUsed`) | `Upgrades.cs` |
| Oaths (Hades' Pact of Punishment), sworn at the temple's oath stone after the first dawn: `OathId`, `Oath`, `Oaths.Rank` (0 while `!Unlocked`), `Heat`, `NoteMultiplier` (applied in `GameManager.Earn`, fractions carried in `noteFraction`), `Cycle`, `ClaimBounty` (in `EndGame` on a dawn, `SaveData.bestHeatCleared`). Effects: `GameManager.Sworn` (march, arrows) on each round's config copy (`LevelConfig.Clone`), `Boss.HealthScale` (set in `StartGame`), `BossPortalAfter` (Long Road), `RollPortals`/`OfferStageBoon` (Faded Spirits), `StartGame` (Frail Body). | The shop page: `ShowSongs`, a two-column `MenuUI` table (any `Item.detail`/`pips` makes a page a table: shorter, wider cells, labels left-aligned, the price right-aligned and the ranks as diamond pips in their own column) with a `MenuUI.Item.note` line for the selected song just under the rows (a rank that asks for a seal or silk shows its price in that colour and names the need and how many he holds). Opened at the temple's flute altar | `GameManager`, `MenuUI` |
| Resources, Hades-style: `ResourceTray` (built in `GameManager.Start`, its own canvas sorted over the menu pages) stacks a count and its looping icon per resource in the bottom-right corner while `state == State.Hub`, station pages included.

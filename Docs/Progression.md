# Across runs: save, dialogue, Flute Songs and Oaths

Part of the [architecture map](../ARCHITECTURE.md). Scripts live under `Assets/Scripts/<System>/`; a bare name like
`Ronin.cs` means the file in its system's folder.

Hades' loop: what happened in earlier runs changes what is said and how strong Oni starts. Research and numbers in
[Story/DIALOGUE_RESEARCH.md](../Story/DIALOGUE_RESEARCH.md) and [ECONOMY.md](ECONOMY.md).

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| The save: runs started and cleared, deaths per boss (`deathsTo`) and wins (`winsVs`) by `BossKind`, how the last run ended (`lastRunCleared`, `lastKiller`, `lastKillerSecondPhase`: the killing boss was in its second phase, from `Boss.SecondPhase`), `hubPending`, conversations heard (`seen`/`seenRun`, the run each was last heard in), the repeatables' `shuffleBag`, `embers` (the Spirit Notes, named for their old Soul Embers so saves keep them), song `ranks` and `songsOff`, and Poguru's tutorial flags `tutorialSettled`/`tutorialReplay`. One JSON string per save slot in `PlayerPrefs` (`"Save1"`..`"Save3"`, see Safe areas), cached in `Save.Data` (reset on entering Play mode, which has no domain reload) | `SaveData.cs` (`Save.BeginRun` in `StartGame`, `Save.EndRun(cleared, killer)` in `EndGame`, `Save.BossFelled` in `BossFell`, `Save.Write` also in `Reload`) |
| Reactive dialogue: a `Conversation` (id, speaker, `DialogueEvent`, `Tier` Story / Reactive / Repeatable, `Req` requirements, `Cooldown` in runs, and either `Lines` or `BuiltIn`, the boss's own `Line()` and clip). `Dialogue.Pick` filters by `Blocked` (heard, cooling down, a `Req` failing), takes the best tier with anything left, the first Story/Reactive one written or a Repeatable from the bag, and marks it heard. No dice in eligibility, so `Explain` is repeatable. Requirements are helpers (`Seen`, `NotSeen`, `LastKiller`, `LastKillerSecondPhase`, `Deaths`, `DeathsTo`, `WinsVs`, `OfElement`, `Holds`, `HoldsDuo`, `Felled` (the boss in `DialogueContext.Boss`), `Met`, `DiedTo` / `DeathsBy` / `Streak` (the run's last blow, below), `OffersBoon`, `NextBoss`, `Most` / `Rival` (another soul's element leads), `LastRunElement`, `AtStage(stage, round)` (`DialogueContext.Stage`/`Round`, from 1), `HeatOn`); `CanPick` checks without marking anything heard; `Pick` can be limited to a tier range so one event's Story lines can be tried before another's; `Fill` writes `{Deaths}` in words. Poguru's events: `Tutorial` (never picked: `Dialogue.Find(id, mark)` plays them by id and marks them heard), `PoguruRound`, `PoguruSoul`, `PoguruBoss`, `PoguruWake` (his roasts, `Dialogue.Roast`, then the gentler wake lines), `PoguruTalk`, `PoguruStage` (a new stage's first rounds, tried before `PoguruRound`), `PoguruScout` (a kite scout appears), `PoguruLowLife` (down to the last heart, once a run, outside boss fights), `PoguruBossAhead` (the boss's path is offered; `NextBoss` is the stage's boss); `GameManager.PoguruBark` plays every line, Oni's over his own head. The souls' boon screens (`GameManager.OfferBoons` → `SoulTalk`, in Story/Souls.md's order): `Legendary`, `Duo` (speaker `"Duo"`), `BossFelled`, `SoulPickup` Story, `BossAhead`, `SoulJealous`, then `SoulPickup`'s reactions and greetings; each talk opens on the soul's sting (`StingFor`, `Sfx.SoulSting*`). `SoulHub`: a soul's line in the temple (`HubReturn`) when Oni has none. The run's last blow: `Ronin.TakeHit(contact, DeathCause)` → `Ronin.LastHitCause` (arrows carry `Arrow.Cause`: Arrow, Spear via `MakeUnbreakable(Sprite)`, GhostArrow for Yurei; dives pass Diver; the army landing is Invasion; any hit in a boss fight is Boss) → `Save.EndRun` (`lastDeathCause`, `deathsByCause`, `deathStreak`, `lastRunElement`) | `Dialogue.cs` (`Dialogue.All` holds every conversation; keep it in step with `Story/`) |
| Where they play: `BeginBoss` picks `BossIntro`, `PlayPhaseCard` picks `BossPhase`, `BossFell` picks `BossDefeat` (none written plays the boss's own line, `Unwritten`), and `OfferBoons` picks the soul's `SoulPickup` (`SoulOf(element)`) on that element's first screen of the run (`metThisRun`) and shows the cards once its scene ends (`GreetThenChoose` → `ShowBoons`). All four play through `PlayScene` (below). `OnRoninHit` picks `KilledOni` from the killing boss (a subtitle; its line is also the end screen text); `EnterSafeArea` runs `HubReturn` when `hubPending`, as he climbs out of the temple's pool (a plain subtitle; `Say` still frames his close-up beside his words if one plays on the title page). `PlayConversation` → `Say` plays lines as subtitles, voiced if `VoiceLibrary` has `Conversation.VoiceId(i)`, and marks them full lines so barks wait | `GameManager` |
| Talk scenes (Hades-style): `PlayScene(conversation)` is `PlayTalk(conversation, default)`; `PlayTalk(said, TalkOptions { keepHud, dim, poguruRight, onLine })` builds the lines (`LinesOf`), and with `keepHud` leaves the HUD exactly as it was (the tutorial's pointing, talks over the boon screen, the temple), `dim` lightens the field, `poguruRight` stands Poguru on the right and Oni on the left, and `onLine(i)` runs as each line comes up (`DialogueScene.Line.shown`). `DialogueScene.Overlay` sits over the plate and figures for what a talk lifts out of the dark, and `Advance()` steps a line for test harnesses. It waits out a hit-stop, sets `State.Dialogue` and `timeScale` 0, hides the HUD and boss bar (`GameUI.HideForScene`), and turns the conversation into `DialogueScene.Line`s: a `BuiltIn` boss line first in its own clip (`BossVoice.Take`), then the written lines with their `VoiceLibrary` clips (a built-in line counts as line 0 of `VoiceId`). `SpeakerFor(name)` stands each speaker: Oni at the right (`oniCloseup`), the boss at the left with its `VoiceFor` close-up (its phase close-up and epithet once `SecondPhase`, drawn at `SceneArtScale` of the card size), a soul at the left with its painting (`akaganeCloseup`…, framed by its eye point) or its soul frames. `DialogueScene.Play` dims the field, lights the speaker and drops the listener back, masks each half (feathered where a painting's canvas edge would show, cut edges pushed off screen, a painting shorter than the screen drawn at screen height so it covers top to bottom), types each line under an ink name tab on the speaker's side, and advances on Space / Enter / click / South after a 0.3 s guard | `DialogueScene.cs`, `GameManager` |
| Voices for written lines: `VoiceLibrary` (every clip in `Audio/Voice` by file name), built by `Ninja/Apply Voice Lines`, which also logs the lines still unvoiced. Oni's lines (hill, answers, soul meetings, `Oni_NotYet`) play through `GameManager.oniVoice`, a filter-free source beside `BossVoice`, the souls' through `soulVoice` (`CreateSoulVoice`: a light chorus and short echo, their shared coffin sound) and Poguru's through `poguruVoice` (`CreatePoguruVoice`, a light quick chorus); `VoiceOf(speaker)` picks the source per line for `DialogueScene.Line.source` and `Say`. An unvoiced intro line stays on the card long enough to read | `VoiceLibrary.cs`, `Editor/VoiceSetup.cs`, `GameUI.PlayBossIntro` |
| The souls behind the boons (Akagane, Mizore, Hibiki): their `SoulPickup` conversations are in `Dialogue.All`; Duo, Legendary, jealousy and boss reactions are written but not wired yet | [Story/Souls.md](../Story/Souls.md) |
| Spirit Notes (`Upgrades.PerSamurai`, `PerScout`, `ForBoss`) go into the save as they're earned (`GameManager.Earn`, `Upgrades.AddNotes`); the end screen tallies `notesThisRun` | `GameManager` |
| Flute Songs: `UpgradeId` (append-only: saves index ranks by it; `Song.Retired` hides one and `RefundRetired` returns its notes, as for Soul Pull), `Song` (name, max rank, base cost, `Effect` text), `Price` (base × rank + ⌊10 × 1.1^ranks bought⌋), `Buy`, `Spent`/`RefundAll`, `Toggle` (maxed songs only), `Rank` (0 while off). Applied once a run, on top of the scene values: `Ronin.LearnSongs` (parry window, speed, swing, shuriken, missed parry, invulnerability), and in `GameManager` Iron Will's life (`StartGame`), Fate's Thread (`redrawsLeft`, `BoonChoiceUI.OfferRedraw`, R) and Banishing Bell (`banishesLeft`, `OfferBanish`, B; the run's `banished` set is skipped by `Boons.Draw`), Third Path (`RollPortals`), Pond's Blessing (`BeadThenNextStage`), Ancestral Lore (`GrantReward`) and Second Dawn (`OnRoninHit`, `dawnUsed`) | `Upgrades.cs` |
| Oaths (Hades' Pact of Punishment), sworn at the temple's oath stone after the first dawn: `OathId`, `Oath`, `Oaths.Rank` (0 while `!Unlocked`), `Heat`, `NoteMultiplier` (applied in `GameManager.Earn`, fractions carried in `noteFraction`), `Cycle`, `ClaimBounty` (in `EndGame` on a dawn, `SaveData.bestHeatCleared`). Effects: `GameManager.Sworn` (march, arrows) on each round's config copy (`LevelConfig.Clone`), `Boss.HealthScale` (set in `StartGame`), `BossPortalAfter` (Long Road), `RollPortals`/`OfferStageBoon` (Faded Spirits), `StartGame` (Frail Body). Page: `ShowOaths` | `Oaths.cs`, `GameManager` |
| The shop page: `ShowSongs`, a two-column `MenuUI` table (any `Item.detail`/`pips` makes a page a table: shorter, wider cells, labels left-aligned, the price right-aligned and the ranks as diamond pips in their own column) with a `MenuUI.Item.note` line for the selected song just under the rows. Opened at the temple's flute altar | `GameManager`, `MenuUI` |
| Resources, Hades-style: `ResourceTray` (built in `GameManager.Start`, its own canvas sorted over the menu pages) stacks a count and its looping icon per resource in the bottom-right corner while `state == State.Hub`, station pages included. Each is one `resources.Add(name, count, icon)` (Spirit Notes: `Save.Data.embers`, `GameManager.NoteIcon`); one at zero stays hidden until found, and a count that changes swells for a moment | `ResourceTray.cs`, `GameManager` |

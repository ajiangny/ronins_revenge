# Kagetsune, the Thousand-Soul Throne

[Back to the story](README.md)

| | |
|---|---|
| Role | The Emperor. Stage 4 final boss (`Assets/Scripts/Bosses/Kagetsune.cs`). Phase 2: **The Unchained Wraith**. |
| Was | A mortal emperor, terrified of dying. |
| Is | Colossal and deathless, inside an armor of a thousand sealed souls. |
| Wants | To stay. Forever, and on his throne. |
| Signature | Bells, the Soul Coffin hammer, teal soul-fire, oni-skull faces. |

His armor is a throne a thousand souls kneel to, which gives him his title. When they turn on him in his second
phase and the coffin's chains snap, he becomes the Unchained Wraith: less an emperor than a storm of the souls he
stole, still wearing his shape.

## Look (from the concept art)

- A colossal horned warlord in blackened scale armor edged in pierced bronze. Curved bronze horns rise from a
  closed, spiked helm, with red eyes burning behind the visor.
- Snarling oni-skull faces on his shoulders, belt and knees, cast to mock Oni after the betrayal.
- Tattered crimson and white robes, strung with paper ofuda talismans and red tasselled cords. Braided red cord
  at his waist.
- **Bronze bells** hang from his waist and robes: one for every sealed soul of rank. The imperial crest is on his
  tasset.
- **Teal soul-fire** pours out of his open chest and wreathes his whole body, thick with drifting skulls.
- **The Soul Coffin:** carried on his shoulder, the coffin-hammer: a great war hammer whose head is a sealed
  coffin-shaped reliquary, chained shut and bound in red cord and ofuda, with an oni face set in it. The shaft is
  wrapped in red cord and ends in a tasselled spike.
- **Phase 2, the Unchained Wraith:** the coffin's chains snap and hang loose, and the armor cracks open into a
  lattice of teal ghost-fire, with screaming faces in his chest. His horns burn, his eyes turn from red to soul
  teal (the souls are looking out now), and skull spirits swarm free all around him.

## History

- **The emperor.** Kagetsune's line had ruled for centuries. He watched his father die slowly and swore it would
  not happen to him.
- **The armor.** He had it forged in secret, around the coffin. It feeds on souls sealed in the coffin, and every
  one rings a bell.
- **The Night of a Thousand Bells.** He called his court, his army and Oni's home village to a coronation at the
  mountain shrine, with Oni as their escort and Benibana dancing her smoke through the hall. Then he opened the
  coffin and drank them all. The last toll stopped the night.
- **Since.** He sends the souls back out each night in borrowed bodies to hunt the one that got away. Every one
  he loses is pulled back into the coffin and forged again, and so is he: the first time Oni kills him, he is
  rebuilt from the souls he still holds.

## His fight

**Stage:** "The Mountain Shrine", the stair up to the hall where the Night of a Thousand Bells was held. Its rounds
bring every kind of soldier and his **Bellringers**, shrine guards whose tolls rouse the army to fire twice as fast;
cutting one down silences its bell. **Arena:** no moon. The coronation courtyard under a starless black sky, lit only
by teal soul-fire braziers and his own chest.

Two bars of 210, the hardest fight in the game, tuned for an Oni carrying a full set of boons. He barely moves: a
few heavy steps that shake the ground. His power is the souls, so he fights as a **bullet hell** of soul-skulls,
always leaving Oni a gap he can reach along his one row. Bronze is a bell Oni can parry to ring a way open
(Resonance); crimson is imperial law, a seal-dart no parry turns.

**Phase 1, the Thousand-Soul Throne.**

| Move | What happens | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Coffer Slam | The hammer marks Oni's lane over three tolls (two lanes past half his bar), then a shockwave runs down it. The first cover in its way takes the blow and breaks; the ground cracks and a slow ring of skulls bursts from it. Past half his bar the mark sometimes jumps onto Oni mid-tell. | The night the coffin opened. |
| Reliquary Rend | Two quick slams on Oni's lane, then a third held overhead for a breath that's never the same length, then the first crack erupts again. | He does not tire, and he does not hurry. |
| Toll of Fealty | Each toll winds a spiral of skulls out of his bells, turning the other way each time; the landings step along Oni's row a gap apart. | The souls answering him. |
| Rosary Curtain | Rows of skulls fall with one gap each, a staircase down. Past half his bar one row has no gap, only a bronze bell to parry. | The court kneeling in rows. |
| Ofuda Decree | Talisman strips drift down into two lanes (three in phase 2), always one of them Oni's. Inside a sealed lane he cannot parry. A slam, a toll, or a rain of crimson seal-darts round skull-walled seals follows. | Imperial law. |
| Thousand-Soul Wail | A beam of soul-fire from his open chest sweeps the field (there and back in phase 2), burning cover. Once a sweep the souls falter and it gutters out; past half his bar it sheds embers. | His power, which is their pain. |
| The Last Toll (parry-only) | A lock-on closes on Oni and a great bronze bell drops on him, steered onto his lane, with an arc of skulls closing in past half his bar. Parried, it rings against the Emperor's armor and staggers him. | His bell turned on him. |

**Phase break.** His bells clamour faster and faster and the coffin's chains snap one by one; the souls tear loose
in a burst of skulls and his eyes turn teal.

**Phase 2, the Unchained Wraith.** Faster, longer combos, skull spirits circling him and his chest-fire swelling as
he weakens, plus:

| Move | What happens |
|---|---|
| Wraith Swarm (the Thousand-Soul Bloom) | A flower of skulls blooms from his chest, halts, and turns on Oni in two volleys. Parried, a skull flies home at him as a freed soul. |
| Soul River | Rows of skulls pour down round one snaking channel, bronze bells on its banks. |
| Broken Chains | The snapped chains slam down either side of Oni and cage him under a skull spiral, then lash across the ground. |
| Coffin Pendulum | He swings the coffin out on its broken chain and scythes the ground down one side of the field, then the other, faster each pass, then brings it down on Oni. |
| Soul Harvest | Rows of souls well up out of the courtyard floor through Oni's row and stream into his chest, each row with one gap; fed, his chest-fire flares. |
| Soul Coffin (parry-only) | He hurls the coffin-hammer down on its chain, seal-darts falling either side. Parried, it bursts against him: a stagger, and he stands open to double damage. |
| Night of a Thousand Bells (every sixth move) | The field goes dark under eight hanging bells and soul-fire falls down every lane but one in five waves, the safe lane stepping aside each time; the last toll is a gapless wall with a bell to parry. Then he kneels, spent and open. |
| **Summoned souls** | He drags a boss out of the coffin, the boss itself at a third of its strength, outlined in his teal fire on a chain from the hammer, and it fights beside him with all of its old first form: Benibana, Utsusemi, Shirogane, one at a time. Cut it down and its chain snaps: the freed soul flies home into his chest like a clapper and rocks him. Leave it standing too long and he drags it back into the coffin ("Mine again.") and wears its power on his next blows: her smoke rings, its hour marks, her ghost swords. Shirogane, bound to his will, can only warn Oni before her blades fall. |
| **The Last Bell** (under 30%) | The dark closes in and pulses with his heart; four spiral arms, thicker blooms, the Night every fourth move, one of its waves leaping two lanes. |

**Defeat.** The coffin bursts. The bells fall silent one by one, each toll lower and quieter than the last, a soul
rising free with each, and the armor sinks hollow and fades. One soul is left kneeling where he fell, still
chained to its bell: Shirogane's, the bell that never rang. Oni cuts the chain; the bell rings once and goes silent,
and she rises as herself. Then the stopped hour ticks on and dawn breaks for the first time in the game: Oni plays
the flute beside her, and she becomes the wisp that settles at his shoulder.

## Voice

His designed ElevenLabs voice: an extremely deep, slow, resonant bass, as if from inside an iron helm, with a choir of
the souls inside him layered underneath (`GameManager.TuneVoice`'s wide, slow chorus and a hall's echo). He never hurries and never shouts until phase 2,
when the chorus starts speaking over him.

## Keep consistent

- No moon motifs: bells, souls, seals and horns are his.
- He never admits fear, except in his defeat line.
- He is the only character who calls Oni "little demon".
- Teal fire is the souls, not his own power. When souls leave him, the fire goes with them.

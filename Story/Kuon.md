## Look

A translucent wolf of blue spirit flame with burning cyan eyes and a mane that rises like fire. It has no shadow and
leaves mist where it steps. Its sprite set (PixelLab character `SpiritWolf`) is idle, howl, charge, vanish and the
Maw (it rears, gathers an orb in its jaws and swallows it).

## History

- **The guardian.** Kuon watched over the shrine of Shirogane's family long before she was born. She grew up
  feeding it rice cakes at the gate. It is not a pet and it is not tame: it chose her.
- **The binding.** When Kagetsune set Shirogane at the Frozen Shrine, he bound Kuon to her soul to make her harder
  to beat. He thought he was giving her a weapon. He gave her a friend.
- **Now.** Kuon fights beside her, on its own ground and its own clock, because that is what a guardian does. It
  doesn't know the fight is a test. It only knows someone is cutting at her. If she falls first, it stops holding
  back.

## Keep consistent

- Kuon obeys Shirogane, never Kagetsune. It is the shrine's guardian, not his creature.
- It never speaks. Its voice is growls and howls (`Sfx.WolfGrowl`, `Sfx.WolfHowl`).
- It isn't killed: broken, it fades, and it is only released at her fall.
